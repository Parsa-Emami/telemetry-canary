# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Keep discussion constructive and focused on the project. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation and sharing another person's private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove offending content or block repeated abuse.

Use a [private maintainer contact](https://github.com/Parsa-Emami) for reports involving people. GitHub's [abuse reporting options](https://support.github.com/report-abuse) are available when appropriate.
